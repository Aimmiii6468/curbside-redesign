In-suite machines

Cheapest per load and always available, if you have them. Most Toronto apartments and older houses do not, and shared basement machines mean queuing and carrying.

Before you book

Eight questions to ask any Toronto laundry service

Including us. If a service will not answer these plainly, that is the answer.

Is the price per pound or per bag?

Per bag sounds simpler and is usually worse. A bag has no fixed weight, so you cannot compare two quotes. Per pound off a scale is the only number you can check.

Is there a minimum, and what is it?

Almost every service has one. Ours is 10 pounds. If a service does not publish its minimum, ask before you book, because a small load can end up costing the minimum anyway.

What is charged up front and what is charged later?

A service that takes the full amount before weighing is guessing. We take $11.30 for pickup and delivery at booking, then charge the wash after it has been on the scale.

Are pickup and delivery charged separately?

Some services charge for each leg. Ask whether the fee covers both the collection and the return, and whether it changes by distance.

Is your postal code actually on the route?

Toronto services advertise the whole city and then serve part of it. Check your first three characters against their real list before you build a plan around it.

Do they tell you before they arrive?

A three hour window is only useful if something reminds you. We send a text and an email one hour before the window opens.

Where is it actually washed?

Ask. Some services ship loads to a central plant in another city. Ours is done at Washworld on Kenwood Avenue in Toronto, a laundromat that has been running for over forty years.

What happens if something is damaged?

Get the answer in writing before you hand over a winter coat. Ours is simple: we re-clean at no charge, or refund in full.

Before you book

Eight questions to ask any Toronto laundry service

Including us. If a service will not answer these plainly, that is the answer.

Is the price per pound or per bag?

Per bag sounds simpler and is usually worse. A bag has no fixed weight, so you cannot compare two quotes. Per pound off a scale is the only number you can check.

Is there a minimum, and what is it?

Almost every service has one. Ours is 10 pounds. If a service does not publish its minimum, ask before you book, because a small load can end up costing the minimum anyway.

What is charged up front and what is charged later?

A service that takes the full amount before weighing is guessing. We take $11.30 for pickup and delivery at booking, then charge the wash after it has been on the scale.

Are pickup and delivery charged separately?

Some services charge for each leg. Ask whether the fee covers both the collection and the return, and whether it changes by distance.

Is your postal code actually on the route?

Toronto services advertise the whole city and then serve part of it. Check your first three characters against their real list before you build a plan around it.

Do they tell you before they arrive?

A three hour window is only useful if something reminds you. We send a text and an email one hour before the window opens.

Where is it actually washed?

Ask. Some services ship loads to a central plant in another city. Ours is done at Washworld on Kenwood Avenue in Toronto, a laundromat that has been running for over forty years.

What happens if something is damaged?

Get the answer in writing before you hand over a winter coat. Ours is simple: we re-clean at no charge, or refund in full.

You do nothing except put a bag at the door. You pay for the collection and for the weight. It wins on time, and on anything too big for a home machine.

Eight questions to ask any Toronto laundry service

Including us. If a service will not answer these plainly, that is the answer.

Is the price per pound or per bag?

Per bag sounds simpler and is usually worse. A bag has no fixed weight, so you cannot compare two quotes. Per pound off a scale is the only number you can check.

Is there a minimum, and what is it?

Almost every service has one. Ours is 10 pounds. If a service does not publish its minimum, ask before you book, because a small load can end up costing the minimum anyway.

What is charged up front and what is charged later?

A service that takes the full amount before weighing is guessing. We take $11.30 for pickup and delivery at booking, then charge the wash after it has been on the scale.

Are pickup and delivery charged separately?

Some services charge for each leg. Ask whether the fee covers both the collection and the return, and whether it changes by distance.

Is your postal code actually on the route?

Toronto services advertise the whole city and then serve part of it. Check your first three characters against their real list before you build a plan around it.

Do they tell you before they arrive?

A three hour window is only useful if something reminds you. We send a text and an email one hour before the window opens.

Where is it actually washed?

Ask. Some services ship loads to a central plant in another city. Ours is done at Washworld on Kenwood Avenue in Toronto, a laundromat that has been running for over forty years.

What happens if something is damaged?

Get the answer in writing before you hand over a winter coat. Ours is simple: we re-clean at no charge, or refund in full.

Process

What we take, how it is washed, and who washes it.9 questions
What types of laundry do you accept?

We accept clothing, bedding, towels, and some delicate items. We do not accept rugs, carpets, or heavily soiled items.

How should I prepare my laundry for pickup?

Please place all items in a securely closed bag. Mark the bag with your name and order number if possible.

What if I have items that require special care?

Please inform us of any special care instructions when scheduling your pickup. We’ll do our best to accommodate your needs.

Do you offer dry cleaning services?

Yes, we offer dry cleaning services. Please indicate which items require dry cleaning when scheduling your pickup.

Where do you process the laundry?

We process your laundry out of Washworld on Kenwood, operating for over 40 years. We have a wide selection of washing machines and dryers with different features.

What detergents do you use?

We offer several options: Free & Clear (hypoallergenic), Standard Clean (fresh scent), or custom requests like no fabric softener or no dryer sheets. Choose during booking!

How do you handle different fabric types?

Our professional staff sorts items by fabric type, color, and care requirements. Delicates get special treatment, and we follow all care label instructions.

Can you handle specialty items?

Yes! We can clean bedding, towels, delicates, and some dry-clean-only items. Let us know about special items when booking, and we’ll provide specific pricing.

How long does the cleaning process take?

Wash and fold take up to 48 hours from pickup to delivery. Dry cleaning can take 7 days.
